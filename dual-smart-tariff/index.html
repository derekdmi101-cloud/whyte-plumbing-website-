Cheap Off-Peak Rates

Smart tariffs like Octopus Agile have prices that vary every 30 minutes. Overnight and at weekends prices often drop below 5p/kWh — sometimes even go negative.

🔋

Store the Heat

Heat your hot water cylinder or buffer tank during cheap slots. A well-insulated cylinder holds heat for 24+ hours — so you're using free (or very cheap) hot water all day.

🤖

Automate It

Pair a Shelly smart relay with this page to automatically switch your immersion heater or heat pump on when prices are low — and off when they spike.

💷

Real Savings

Customers shifting just 60% of their hot water heating to off-peak are saving £200–£600/year. Heat pump users save significantly more.
